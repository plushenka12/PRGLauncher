# macOS smoke checklist

- Open the unsigned universal build using the documented Gatekeeper override.
- Verify menu-bar icon, mini-bar position, always-on-top behavior and traffic lights.
- Start PRGLauncher without Wi-Fi, reconnect later and verify sync retries automatically.
- Sign in with two accounts in sequence and confirm profile isolation.
- Test Steam import/launch, Nintendo sync window, HLTB lookup and RAWG covers.
- Open Game Info and Settings; verify focus, scrolling and close behavior.
- Export/import a backup and verify sessions and Recap data.
- Check updater UI and confirm the app exits before an update is installed.
